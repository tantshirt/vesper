# Persona 4 — Kai, the Crypto-Native Skeptic

**Age:** 25–35 · **Priority:** 4 (edge / credibility segment)

> *"Most 'RWA' is TradFi cosplay with extra steps. Prove it's on-chain, prove it's real, and let me hold my own keys."*

## Profile
Owns SOL, uses Phantom, Jupiter, Kamino. Fluent in wallets, DvP, custody. Deeply skeptical of tokenized-real-world claims. Small in number but **loud and credibility-lending** — the segment whose proof demands raise the transparency bar for everyone. Won't drive volume, but winning them de-risks the brand.

## Positive driving forces (WANTS)
- **On-chain yield backed by *real* assets**
- **Verifiable proof** — mint, holder view, DvP receipts, explorer links
- **Self-custody + export** (no black box)
- **USDC-native** flows

## Negative driving forces (FEARS)
- **Fake / synthetic "RWA"** with no real backing (strongest)
- **Opaque middlemen** / "TradFi cosplay"
- **Custodial black boxes**
- **No real exit**

## Design implications
On-chain receipts, external-wallet connect via Privy, Token ACL disclosure, exportable history, sober compliance-gated secondary market. His demands justify the **"provable on demand"** layer beneath P1's invisible-by-default surface — the key tension the Property Detail screen must resolve.
