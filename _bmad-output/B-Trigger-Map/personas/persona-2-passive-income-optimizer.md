# Persona 2 — Priya, the Passive Income Optimizer

**Age:** 28–40 · **Priority:** 2 (secondary)

> *"Show me reliable income and clean numbers. If a distribution is late or my taxes are a mess, I'm out."*

## Profile
Established, disciplined with money. Already uses HYSAs, index ETFs, robo-advisors, maybe some stablecoin yield. Optimizes for predictable income and low admin. Brings **larger check sizes and repeat behavior** — the engine behind objectives O2 (volume) and O4 (repeat rate + reliability).

## Positive driving forces (WANTS)
- **Predictable recurring income**
- **Clean reporting & tax clarity**
- **Diversification / allocation control**
- **Set-and-forget** (auto-reinvest)

## Negative driving forces (FEARS)
- **Missed or unreliable distributions** (strongest)
- **Messy tax admin**
- Yield that's **risk in disguise**
- **Concentrated, correlated loss**

## Design implications
Income calendar, distribution-reliability signals, monthly update feed, exposure/concentration visibility, tax center, auto-reinvest (post-MVP). Her fears validate the **yield breakdown** and **on-time distributions** commitments. She's why O4 and the ≤35% concentration cap matter.
