# Persona 1 — Maya, the First-Job Wealth Builder  ⭐ PRIMARY

**Age:** 22–29 · **Priority:** 1 (primary — 5× design weight)

> *"I finally have a little money saved. I want it to actually build something — but I can't afford a house, and I don't trust anything that feels like a scam."*

## Profile
Early career, first real paycheck, a few hundred to a few thousand saved. Lives in money apps — Cash App, Robinhood, Coinbase, Wise. Financially curious but not sophisticated; learns by doing in-app. Time-poor, mobile-only. Under Reg A+ she is fully eligible — she *is* the "anyone can own" vision.

## Positive driving forces (WANTS)
- Own something **real and tangible** — "my first square foot"
- Build wealth **without a mortgage** or gatekeepers
- Feel financially **on-track / adult**
- An entry small enough to **afford now** ($50–100)

## Negative driving forces (FEARS) — design against these first
- **Losing hard-earned savings to a scam** (strongest)
- Crypto complexity that makes her **feel dumb**
- Being **locked out of real estate** forever
- **Hidden risk** she can't see or understand

## Design implications
Plain-language everything; "add money" not "fund wallet"; the **"What you own" card** and **risk cards + diligence gates** answer her scam fear directly; the **calculator** de-risks her first commit; crypto is **invisible** (Privy embedded wallet). She is the target for the O3 comprehension metric.
