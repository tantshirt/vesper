# Persona 3 — Devin, the Rent-Trapped Aspirational Buyer

**Age:** 24–34 · **Priority:** 3 (tertiary)

> *"I'm priced out of buying in my city. At least let me own a piece of the market I'm stuck renting in."*

## Profile
Rents in an expensive metro, watches home prices climb out of reach. Wants exposure to real-estate appreciation *before* a down payment is realistic. Psychologically adjacent to P1 (same "wealth without a mortgage" spine) — widens the top of the funnel.

## Positive driving forces (WANTS)
- **Appreciation exposure before** they can buy
- "**Build a portfolio before buying property**"
- Feeling they're **catching up**, not falling behind
- Understand **neighborhoods & markets**

## Negative driving forces (FEARS)
- **Priced out forever**
- Watching **others build wealth** while they rent
- Making a **naive first-investment mistake**

## Design implications
Neighborhood/market explainers, property risk score, "start building before you buy" framing, the **Learn tab**, and the same calculator + risk cards that reassure P1. Acquisition-adjacent to P1; shares most MUST-tier features, so serving P1 largely serves Devin.
