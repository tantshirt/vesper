# Persona 4 — Ken, the Sponsor Principal

**Role:** External sponsor / operator principal (B2B supply) · **Priority:** 4

> *"I have a real building and real numbers. Tell me exactly what you need, treat my data as mine, and don't make me chase you."*

## Profile
Property operator deciding to bring a deal to Vesper. Must pass **Gate 0 (KYB + UBO)** before anything lists. Signs the legal docs. Lives entirely in the **walled sponsor portal** — sees only his own funding/holder view, **no internal surfaces, no other sponsors**. Time-poor, deal-focused, allergic to opaque process.

## Positive driving forces (WANTS)
- A **clear intake checklist** — exactly what's required, up front
- **Fast, predictable diligence** with no moving goalposts
- His sensitive data kept **private and tenant-isolated**
- **Visibility** into his offering's funding progress + holders once live

## Negative driving forces (FEARS) — design against these first
- An **opaque, slow, goalpost-moving** process (strongest)
- His **sensitive financials leaking** to competitors or other sponsors
- Being **nickel-and-dimed** on fees with no clarity
- **Not knowing where his deal stands**

## Design implications
Guided **intake with explicit requirements + live status timeline**; **strict tenant isolation** (a sponsor can never see another sponsor's data or any internal surface); a clear **diligence status** view (what's passed, what's pending, what's needed from him); **e-signature** flows for legal docs; a funding/holder dashboard post-listing. Fee terms shown plainly. Note the integrity wall: **his listing fee never touches a gate decision** (I5) — and he never sees gate-decision internals.
