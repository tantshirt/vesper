# Persona 5 — Sofia, the Sponsor Ops Lead

**Role:** External sponsor ops / finance staff · **Priority:** 5

> *"I'm the one uploading the documents and posting the monthly update. Make it obvious what's missing, and remind me before I'm late."*

## Profile
Runs the sponsor's day-to-day on Vesper: intake + diligence submission, responding to gate requests, and authoring **the monthly property update the consumer is promised**. Uploads documents, chases her own internal teams for them. **No gate sign-off, no settlement access.** Detail-oriented, deadline-driven, hates redoing work.

## Positive driving forces (WANTS)
- An **obvious "what's missing" checklist** with validation on upload
- **Easy document upload** that tells her immediately if something's wrong
- **Reminders before** a monthly update is due
- A **fast, plain-language path** to answer a gate request

## Negative driving forces (FEARS) — design against these first
- Submitting the **wrong or incomplete document** and not knowing (strongest)
- **Missing a monthly-update deadline** — which breaks the consumer trust promise (O5)
- A **gate request she can't understand** (jargon, unclear ask)
- **Redoing work** because of a preventable error

## Design implications
**Completeness checklists + validation on upload** (reject the wrong doc early); a **monthly-update composer** with due-date reminders that **feeds the ops overdue-flagging** system; **plain-language gate requests** (the internal ask translated for the sponsor); clear **resubmission loops** with status. She is where the "≥95% on-time monthly updates" objective (AO4) is won or lost.
