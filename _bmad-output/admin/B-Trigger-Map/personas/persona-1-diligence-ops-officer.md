# Persona 1 — Priya, the Diligence / Ops Officer  ⭐ PRIMARY

**Role:** Internal supply & operations lead · **Priority:** 1 (primary — 5× design weight)

> *"I'm the name on the gate. If a bad property gets through, that's on me — so put the evidence in front of me, make the safe path the fast path, and never let me fat-finger something irreversible."*

## Profile
Runs a property through its whole life: submitted → gated → minted → listed → paying. Reviews AI-assembled evidence, **signs gates as a named human**, and operates the mint and distribution consoles. Desktop-bound, multi-tab, throughput-pressured but accountability-first. She **is** the ops spine — the admin equivalent of Maya's signature journey (gate → mint → distribute).

## Positive driving forces (WANTS)
- Move **real, well-evidenced deals** through **fast**
- Evidence and source docs **at her fingertips** at the moment of signing
- The **safe action to be the default** — hard to do the dangerous thing by accident
- To be able to **prove any decision later** (who, what, why, when)

## Negative driving forces (FEARS) — design against these first
- **Signing off on something fake or unfit** (strongest) — the supply-side scam fear
- An **irreversible on-chain mistake** — minting or distributing the wrong thing
- A **segregation-of-duties violation on her watch** (touching a gate she has a fee stake in)
- **Silent drift** between chain and Convex she didn't catch

## Design implications
Evidence-linked **gate signature ceremony**; **consequence + cost + finality stated before** any irreversible on-chain action; SoD conflicts render as **hard blocks with the reason + the required second signer**; reconciliation status **always visible**; the champagne "star" is reserved for a genuinely earned moment — a gate **fully** signed, a distribution **pushed and reconciled**. She is the target for the supply-integrity objective (AO1).
