## Reading the scores

- **The integrity quartet tops the ranking** (Gate ceremony, Segregation-of-duties, Sponsor intake, AuditLog = ranks 1–4). These sit on the fears **every internal operator shares** — the accountability mirror of the consumer trust quartet. Independent confirmation that supply integrity + provability earn MVP priority.
- **\* Mint & Distribution consoles score 7, not top-tier — but stay MUST.** This is a **scoring artifact**, the exact analog of the consumer's *"What you own"* card (which scored 8 yet stayed MUST): only P1 rates them HIGH because the acute accountability lives with the operator, while others rate MED/LOW. Strategically they **are** the build spine (gate → mint → distribute) — so they override pure score → **MUST**. *Score informs, it does not dictate.*
- **† AI extraction/flag review scores 7 but is the mechanism behind AO5** (zero AI-authored approvals). The *guarantee* is the human signature (rank 1); this feature is *how* the AI stays isolated and useful. MUST for the diligence epic.
- **‡ Compliance console + Marketing sign-off score 6 but are non-negotiable** — legal/Reg A+ requirements, not discretionary. **MUST** regardless of score (mirrors the consumer KYC/ACL note).
- **§ RBAC/role management scores 5 but is foundational** — every other feature's segregation guarantee depends on it. **MUST** as substrate (belongs in the foundation epic), even though only P6 rates it HIGH.
- **Sponsor-only + monitoring features cluster mid/low** (funding dashboard, continuous monitoring, portfolio-risk). Correct for MVP sequencing: they serve single-role *wants* or advanced/edge needs, so they're SHOULD/COULD without weakening the spine.
