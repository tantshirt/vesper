## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Let Maya choose *how much* to invest and see — before she commits — exactly what she'll own, what she'll earn (base **and** downside), and the full cost. De-risk the decision by making every number legible and honest.

**User Situation:** She's decided on The Monroe from Property Detail, is verified and funded, and now sets an amount. Guarded about hidden costs and "what if it drops."

**Success Criteria:** She sets an amount, understands ownership %, monthly income, first-year base & downside, and the one-time fee — then taps through to review with confidence. Feeds O3 (comprehension) + O2 (funded).

**Entry:** Invest CTA on Property Detail (01.2).
**Exit:** "Review rights & risks" → 01.7 (then atomic DvP in 01.8). Back → Property Detail.

## Key Objects

### `calc-context` — property mini-header
Small, persistent reminder of what's being bought (name · target yield · location). Tap → back to Property Detail.

### `calc-amount` — the calculator input
- **Big editable amount** (Inter 600 tabular, indigo). Direct entry + **slider** ($50–$2,000) + **quick chips** ($50 / $100 / $250 / $500).
- Min $50 enforced (matches brief); below shows inline "The minimum is $50."
- Every downstream number recomputes live on change.

### `calc-projection` — what you'd get (Base | Downside toggle)
- **You'd own:** `amount / offering_size` → %. (Offering basis: appraisal $1,240,000.)
- **Est. monthly income:** `amount × 4.8% ÷ 12` (rent is the cash-distributed part).
- **First year (Base):** `amount × 6.2%` = rent (4.8%) + modeled appreciation (1.4%), itemized.
- **Downside toggle (−12% value):** first-year becomes `≈ amount × −0.8%` — shown in loss color with a `−`. This is the "sees downside scenario" beat from S1. Never hidden behind a tap-away.
- Rule: "targets/estimates," never "guaranteed."

### `calc-order` — order preview
- Investment · **one-time platform fee 0.9%** (disclosed here, before confirm — per brief revenue model) · total today · funding source · resulting ownership + the SPV name.
- Management fee is *not* re-charged here — it's already in the 6.2% net (state this).

### `calc-cta` — sticky
"Review rights & risks →" leads to 01.7 acknowledgement, then atomic DvP (01.8). Solid indigo (design-system primary). Never "Buy tokens."

## States

| State | When | Behavior |
|-------|------|----------|
| Default | Amount ≥ $50 | All projections live; CTA enabled |
| Below minimum | Amount < $50 | Inline hint; CTA disabled |
| Insufficient funds | Amount > USDC balance | "Add $X more" inline → 01.5 Add Money, preserves amount |
| Downside view | Toggle = Downside | First-year figure flips to loss color + `−`; copy explains the −12% assumption |
| Recalculating | On input | Numbers update instantly, tabular, no layout shift |
