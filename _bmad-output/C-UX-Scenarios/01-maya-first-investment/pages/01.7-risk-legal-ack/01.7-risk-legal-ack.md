## Metadata
Scenario 01 · Flow step (gate before execution) · Mobile PWA · Authenticated · Tokens: [D-Design-System](../../../D-Design-System/00-design-system.md)

## Overview
**Purpose:** The binding risk & legal acknowledgement before atomic DvP — make the user *actively* confirm they understand what they own, that returns can fall, and that liquidity isn't guaranteed. Comprehension gate, not fine print. **Success:** all acknowledgements checked → Confirm enabled → executes. **Exit:** Confirm → atomic DvP settle → 01.8.

## Key objects
- `ack-summary` — order recap (property · target yield · ownership % · total today · "settles instantly, in USDC"), mirrors the calculator numbers.
- `ack-checks` — **three required acknowledgements** (each must be actively checked):
  1. "I'm buying a share in the LLC that owns this building — not a savings account or guaranteed return."
  2. "Rent can fall or pause; I could get back less than I put in."
  3. "Liquidity isn't guaranteed; selling may take time."
- `ack-cta` — "Confirm & invest" — **disabled until all three checked**; triggers the atomic DvP settle (payment + asset delivery together or fail together).

## States
Default (CTA disabled) · Partial (some checked) · Ready (all checked → CTA enabled) · Settling (brief "Settling…" state) · Failure (DvP fails atomically → nothing charged, clear retry).

## Notes
This is the "binding risk & legal acknowledgement" beat from S1. Layered risk disclosure principle: plain acknowledgements here link to full risk factors. The AI is never involved in approval; these are the user's own attestations.
