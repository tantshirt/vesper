# 01.8-confirmation — "You're an owner"

**Previous:** ← [01.7 Rights & Risks](../01.7-risk-legal-ack/01.7-risk-legal-ack.md) · **Next:** → Portfolio (02.x) / Explore

## Metadata
Scenario 01 · Flow endpoint (the payoff) · Mobile PWA · Authenticated · Tokens: [D-Design-System](../../../D-Design-System/00-design-system.md)

## Overview
**Purpose:** The emotional payoff — turn an abstract "investment" into felt ownership the instant atomic DvP settles. Answer, warmly and concretely, "what did I just get?" **Success:** the user understands exactly what they own, when they'll be paid, and that it's real & on-chain — the O3 comprehension moment. **Exit:** View portfolio · See on-chain proof · Back to Explore.

## Key objects
- `conf-mark` — a dusk medallion with the champagne **star/keystone** (the one evening-star moment; the brand mark as the success symbol).
- `conf-headline` — "You're an owner." (Fraunces) + "Your share of The Monroe is yours — recorded on-chain, settled in USDC."
- `conf-owner-card` — dusk header "You now own **0.0081% of The Monroe**"; rows: invested · est. first rent · **first distribution date (Aug 1)** · **DvP settlement receipt (0x…)**.
- `conf-links` — "View in your portfolio ›" · "See the on-chain proof ›" (mint · receipt — provable on demand, for the skeptic).
- `conf-closing` — "Rent arrives while you sleep. We'll let you know the moment your first distribution lands." (brief voice).

## States
Success (default) · pending-settlement (rare; "confirming on-chain…") — but DvP is atomic so this is brief.

## Notes
Numbers mirror the calculator/rights (amount, ownership %, first-rent estimate carry through). This screen sets up S2 (first-payout check-in). The champagne star appears here as the earned success moment — the single evening star for this screen.
