# 01.1-explore

**Next Step:** → [01.2 Property Detail](../01.2-property-detail/01.2-property-detail.md)

## Metadata
Scenario 01 (front door; also S3, S5) · Full page · Mobile PWA · **Public** (browse without auth) · Tokens: [D-Design-System](../../../D-Design-System/00-design-system.md)

## Overview
**Purpose:** The front door — let anyone browse vetted, income-producing properties with zero friction and pick one to explore. **Success:** visitor opens a Property Detail with intent. **Entry:** app open / Home tab / deep link. **Exit:** tap a property card → 01.2.

## Key objects
- `explore-greet` — "Good evening, Maya · Own your first square foot." (evening motif, Fraunces).
- `explore-filters` — chips: All / Monthly income / Lower risk / $50 min.
- `explore-pcard` (repeats) — dusk thumbnail (one lit window), **funding-% badge**, **yield pill**, name (Fraunces), location·type·units, tag pills ($50 min / income / risk), funding progress bar + "$X raised · N%". From brief: **The Monroe 6.2% / Cedar Row 5.7% / Alder Court 6.8%**.
- `explore-tabbar` — Home · Explore · Portfolio · Market · Learn (the five tabs; no Wallet tab per brief).

## States
Default (list) · Loading (card skeletons, no CLS) · Empty ("New properties drop regularly — check back") · Funding closed (card shows "Fully funded", still viewable).

## Notes
Public + SSR, but **Reg A+ solicitation limits** govern how much offering detail renders pre-auth (open question, shared with 01.2). Cards are the primary conversion surface — keep yield honest (target, with risk tag), never a lone number.
