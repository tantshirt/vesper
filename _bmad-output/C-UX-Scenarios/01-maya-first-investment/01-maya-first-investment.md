# 01: Maya's First Investment  ★ signature journey

**Project:** Vesper
**Created:** 2026-07-07
**Method:** Web Design Studio (WDS)

---

## Transaction (Q1)

A first-time investor goes from "I heard you can own a piece of real estate" to actually owning a fractional share of a specific, verified property — understanding what she owns before she confirms.

---

## Business Goal (Q2)

**Goal:** Get real people to fund real properties (O2 growth) while proving they understand what they bought (O3 comprehension).
**Objective:** O2 (500–1,500 funded investors) + O3 (≥90% can answer "what do I own?") — the north-star runs through this scenario.

---

## User & Situation (Q3)

**Persona:** Maya, the First-Job Wealth Builder (**Primary**)
**Situation:** 25, two years into her first real job, ~$1,800 saved beyond her emergency fund. She's on the train, phone in hand, having seen a friend's post about owning "a piece of a rental." She's curious but guarded — she's seen crypto rug-pulls and doesn't want to look naïve or lose money she worked hard for.

---

## Driving Forces (Q4)

**Hope:** To own something real — her "first square foot" — and finally feel like she's building wealth.

**Worry:** That it's a scam, or so crypto-complicated she'll feel stupid and lose her savings.

---

## Device & Starting Point (Q5 + Q6)

**Device:** Mobile (primary)
**Entry:** Taps a shared link from a friend / an app-store install; lands on a property teaser she can browse **without** signing up or KYC.

---

## Best Outcome (Q7)

**User Success:** Maya owns a fractional share of a specific property for as little as $50, can state in her own words what she owns, where her return comes from, and how she'd exit — and feels calm, not confused.

**Business Success:** A funded primary investment with KYC + Reg A+ eligibility recorded, atomic DvP settled, and a post-invest comprehension check passed (feeds O3).

---

## Shortest Path (Q8)

*Linear sunshine path — no branches.*

1. **Explore** — browses properties freely; taps one that looks approachable.
2. **Property Detail ★** — reads the *"What you own"* card, the yield breakdown, the Liquidity Reality Box, and the risk cards + passed diligence gates. Decides to invest.
3. **Signup (Privy)** — passkey/social login; embedded wallet pre-generated invisibly.
4. **Identity & Eligibility** — quick KYC; Reg A+ per-investor limit shown plainly, not as a wall.
5. **Add Money** — card/ACH funds USDC behind a fiat-like flow ("Add $100").
6. **Investment Flow — calculator + order preview** — sets amount ($50–100), sees projected monthly income and downside scenario, reviews order.
7. **Risk & legal acknowledgement** — plain-language rights + risk confirmation before execution.
8. **Confirmation / rights summary** — atomic DvP settles; "You own 0.018% of 214 Oak St. First payout expected next month." ✓
