# 04: Kai Verifies the Proof

**Project:** Vesper
**Created:** 2026-07-07
**Method:** Web Design Studio (WDS)

---

## Transaction (Q1)

A skeptical crypto-native investor pressure-tests whether the "real-world asset" is actually real and on-chain — inspecting the mint, the receipts, and self-custody — before he'll trust it with a dollar.

---

## Business Goal (Q2)

**Goal:** Win the hardest critics so their validation becomes credibility (supports O2 via trust halo) and raise the proof bar for everyone.
**Objective:** O1 (verified supply, all gates signed) made *visible* + O2 (conversion of high-credibility users).

---

## User & Situation (Q3)

**Persona:** Kai, the Crypto-Native Skeptic (**Edge / credibility**)
**Situation:** 29, holds SOL, runs Phantom and Jupiter, has been burned by "RWA" projects that were vapor. He's poking at Vesper expecting to find the catch — an off-chain black box, no real backing, or custody he can't control.

---

## Driving Forces (Q4)

**Hope:** On-chain yield genuinely backed by a real asset, with proof he can independently verify and keys he can hold.

**Worry:** Fake/synthetic "RWA," opaque middlemen ("TradFi cosplay"), a custodial black box, or no real exit.

---

## Device & Starting Point (Q5 + Q6)

**Device:** Mobile + desktop (he'll open an explorer in another tab)
**Entry:** Arrives from a crypto-community link, lands directly on a Property Detail, and immediately digs past the polished surface looking for proof.

---

## Best Outcome (Q7)

**User Success:** Kai follows the token to its Token-2022 mint on-chain, sees holder count and a DvP receipt, confirms the legal wrapper and passed diligence gates, connects his own Phantom wallet, and confirms he can export/self-custody. The catch he expected isn't there.

**Business Success:** A converted skeptic who vouches publicly; the transparency layer proven under adversarial inspection.

---

## Shortest Path (Q8)

1. **Property Detail** — skips past the plain-language card to the evidence: legal wrapper, passed diligence gates, on-chain mint reference.
2. **On-chain Proof / receipts** — taps through to the Token-2022 mint, holder view, DvP receipt, and explorer links; reads the Token ACL disclosure.
3. **External wallet connect** — connects Phantom via Privy; confirms self-custody + export path.
4. **Market (secondary)** — checks the sober, compliance-gated secondary listing to confirm a *real* exit exists (with honest liquidity warnings). ✓
