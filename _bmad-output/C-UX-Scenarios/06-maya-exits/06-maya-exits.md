# 06: Maya Exits

**Project:** Vesper
**Created:** 2026-07-07
**Method:** Web Design Studio (WDS)

---

## Transaction (Q1)

An investor needs her money back and tests whether she can actually get out — experiencing Vesper's honest liquidity design instead of a false "instant sell" promise.

---

## Business Goal (Q2)

**Goal:** Build durable trust by being honest about liquidity (O5 trust cadence) — the anti-RealT commitment — so investors stay even when they can't exit instantly.
**Objective:** O5 (trust) + secondary-market integrity; protects the whole brand's "liquidity honesty" principle.

---

## User & Situation (Q3)

**Persona:** Maya, the First-Job Wealth Builder (**Primary**)
**Situation:** Six months in, Maya has an unexpected expense and wonders if she can sell part of her holding. She's braced for the classic trap — "your money is locked and nobody told you."

---

## Driving Forces (Q4)

**Hope:** To get some money back reasonably, understanding realistically what to expect.

**Worry:** That she's trapped, that there's a hidden lockup, or that "sell" is a lie.

---

## Device & Starting Point (Q5 + Q6)

**Device:** Mobile
**Entry:** Opens the app with intent to sell; goes to her Portfolio.

---

## Best Outcome (Q7)

**User Success:** Maya sees the honest Liquidity Reality Box — current bids, last sale, estimated time-to-fill, spread, any lockup — lists a portion at a realistic price with clear expectations, and never feels lied to (whether or not it fills immediately).

**Business Success:** Trust preserved through an honest exit experience; a compliance-gated secondary listing created via DvP; no "instant liquidity" expectation was ever set.

---

## Shortest Path (Q8)

1. **Portfolio** — selects the holding she wants to reduce.
2. **Market (secondary)** — enters the sober, compliance-gated secondary surface.
3. **Liquidity Reality Box** — reads current bids, last sale, estimated exit time, spread, lockup, transfer eligibility — set honestly, no "Sell now = instant cash" framing.
4. **List / sell order** — lists a portion at a realistic price with a clear, expectation-setting confirmation (may or may not fill immediately). ✓

---

## Trigger Map Connections

**Persona:** Maya (Primary)

**Driving Forces Addressed:**
- ✅ **Want:** Confidence that ownership isn't a trap; a real path to her money.
- ❌ **Fear:** "Can I get out?" — being locked in, hidden lockups, a fake "sell."

**Business Goal:** O5 (trust/honesty) — the third of the three core fears, given its own journey.

> This scenario exists to make the **"Can I get out?"** answer honest and visible — the RealT cautionary tale designed against, in UX form.
