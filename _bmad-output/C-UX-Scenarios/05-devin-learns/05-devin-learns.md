# 05: Devin Learns Before Buying

**Project:** Vesper
**Created:** 2026-07-07
**Method:** Web Design Studio (WDS)

---

## Transaction (Q1)

A rent-trapped aspiring buyer who feels priced out uses Vesper to *understand* real-estate investing and a specific market first, building the confidence to make a small, informed first move.

---

## Business Goal (Q2)

**Goal:** Widen the top of the funnel (O2) by converting education into confident first investments, and lift comprehension (O3).
**Objective:** O2 (verified users → funded investors) + O3 (comprehension).

---

## User & Situation (Q3)

**Persona:** Devin, the Rent-Trapped Aspirational Buyer (**Tertiary**)
**Situation:** 31, rents in a city where buying feels impossible. He's motivated but under-informed — worried he'll make a naive mistake, but more worried about staying on the sidelines while everyone else builds wealth.

---

## Driving Forces (Q4)

**Hope:** To get real-estate exposure *before* he can buy — to start catching up.

**Worry:** Being priced out forever, or fumbling a first investment out of ignorance.

---

## Device & Starting Point (Q5 + Q6)

**Device:** Mobile
**Entry:** Arrives via an educational/social post ("build a property portfolio before you buy property") and lands on the **Learn** tab.

---

## Best Outcome (Q7)

**User Success:** Devin understands how fractional ownership, returns, and risk work, reads a neighborhood/market explainer, and looks at a property's risk cards with informed eyes — feeling capable rather than intimidated (and set up to become a first-time investor).

**Business Success:** An educated, higher-intent lead progressing toward a funded first investment; comprehension raised before money moves.

---

## Shortest Path (Q8)

1. **Learn** — plain-language education: what you own, where returns come from, how exit works, and the honest risks.
2. **Neighborhood / market explainer** — a market deep-dive + property risk-score explanation that demystifies "is this a good area?"
3. **Explore** — browses properties now with a frame for evaluating them.
4. **Property Detail — risk cards** — reads the layered risk disclosures and passed diligence gates with newly-informed eyes. ✓
