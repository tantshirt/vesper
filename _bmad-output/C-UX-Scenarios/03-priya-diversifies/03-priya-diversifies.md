# 03: Priya Diversifies & Reviews Reliability

**Project:** Vesper
**Created:** 2026-07-07
**Method:** Web Design Studio (WDS)

---

## Transaction (Q1)

A disciplined income investor adds a second property to spread risk, checks that her income is reliable and her exposure isn't concentrated, and confirms the tax picture is clean.

---

## Business Goal (Q2)

**Goal:** Grow funded volume and repeat rate through higher-value, retention-prone investors (O2, O4) while honoring the ≤35% concentration cap (O5).
**Objective:** O2 (funded volume), O4 (repeat rate), O5 (no market >35% exposure).

---

## User & Situation (Q3)

**Persona:** Priya, the Passive Income Optimizer (**Secondary**)
**Situation:** 34, already holds one Vesper property plus HYSAs and ETFs. It's a Sunday; she's doing her monthly money review and wants to add exposure — but only if the reporting is clean and she's not doubling down on one market.

---

## Driving Forces (Q4)

**Hope:** Predictable, diversified income with reporting she can trust at tax time.

**Worry:** Missed/unreliable distributions, messy tax admin, or accidentally concentrating in one market.

---

## Device & Starting Point (Q5 + Q6)

**Device:** Mobile → occasionally desktop for review
**Entry:** Opens the app directly from her monthly-review routine; heads to Explore with an allocation mindset.

---

## Best Outcome (Q7)

**User Success:** Priya funds a second property in a *different* market, sees her blended yield and that no single market exceeds 35%, and downloads a clean tax summary. She trusts the machine.

**Business Success:** A larger, repeat investment; diversification that reduces portfolio risk; a retained high-value investor.

---

## Shortest Path (Q8)

1. **Explore (allocation lens)** — filters by market/type/risk; picks a property in a market she doesn't yet hold.
2. **Property Detail — yield breakdown (deep)** — scrutinizes rent vs appreciation vs fees vs reserves vs debt; confirms the management/platform fees are disclosed.
3. **Investment Flow** — funds the position (reuses saved funding + existing eligibility).
4. **Portfolio — allocation & concentration** — sees blended income, allocation by market/type, and the ≤35% exposure indicator stays green.
5. **Tax center** — downloads/preview a clean tax summary (placeholder-grade in MVP, but honest about it). ✓

---
