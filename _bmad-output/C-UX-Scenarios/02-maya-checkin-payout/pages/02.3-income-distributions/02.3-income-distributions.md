## Metadata
Scenario 02 (also S3 Priya) · Per-holding income view · Mobile PWA · Authenticated · Tokens: [D-Design-System](../../../D-Design-System/00-design-system.md)

## Overview
**Purpose:** Make the payout *understood*, not just received — the O3 north-star. Break this month's distribution into its parts and confirm it matches the target. **Success:** Maya can say where her $3.42 came from and that it's on schedule. **Exit:** → property update (02.4) / back.

## Key objects
- `inc-breakdown` — this month: **gross rent share → operating costs → management fee (Vesper) → reserve → net paid in USDC.** Every line itemized (mirrors the property's yield breakdown).
- `inc-match` — reassurance chip: "✓ Matches your 6.2% target — on schedule." (Directly serves the "will I get paid / is it what was promised" fear.)
- `inc-history` — income calendar: month-by-month distributions (tabular, gain color) + **next distribution date**.

## States
Default · first-distribution (celebratory framing) · **missed/paused** (honest: explain why, link to update; never silent) · below-target (explain variance calmly).

## Notes
This is the comprehension screen — the north-star metric lives here. The "matches your target" confirmation is the payoff of the calculator's earlier projection. For P4, this + tax center is the reporting backbone.
