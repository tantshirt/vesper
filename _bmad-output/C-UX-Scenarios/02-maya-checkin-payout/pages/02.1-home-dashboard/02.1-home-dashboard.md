# 02.1-home-dashboard

**Next:** → [02.2 Portfolio](../02.2-portfolio/02.2-portfolio.md)

## Metadata
Scenario 02 · Home tab (default landed screen) · Mobile PWA · Authenticated · Tokens: [D-Design-System](../../../D-Design-System/00-design-system.md)

## Overview
**Purpose:** Answer "How am I doing?" in one glance and make the *payout the hero* the month it lands. **Success:** Maya immediately sees rent arrived, her value, and this month's income — and feels proof. **Entry:** app open / push notification ("Your rent from The Monroe has arrived"). **Exit:** → Portfolio / a property.

## Key objects
- `home-greet` — "Good evening, Maya · How you're doing." (evening motif, Fraunces).
- `home-landed` ★ — dusk card with champagne star: **"Rent just landed · +$3.42 · from The Monroe."** The emotional hero; only shown when a distribution is fresh.
- `home-balance` — portfolio value (Inter tabular), all-time return, **balance sparkline** (SVG, champagne endpoint), + invested / income-to-date / this-month grid.
- `home-properties` — mini holdings list (The Monroe, Cedar Row) → tap into portfolio/property.
- `home-tabbar` — five tabs, Home active.

## States
Default · **fresh-distribution** (landed card visible) · no-distribution-yet (card replaced by "next distribution Aug 1") · empty (pre-first-investment → "Own your first square foot" nudge → Explore).

## Notes
The landed card is the S2 hero — the moment the product exists for. Champagne star = the one evening-star accent here. Numbers mirror the portfolio/income screens.
