# 02: Maya Checks In & Gets Her First Payout

**Project:** Vesper
**Created:** 2026-07-07
**Method:** Web Design Studio (WDS)

---

## Transaction (Q1)

An existing investor returns to see how she's doing and experiences her first real distribution — the moment that turns an abstract "investment" into felt ownership.

---

## Business Goal (Q2)

**Goal:** Convert first-time investors into repeat, retained investors (O4) by making income real, reliable, and legible.
**Objective:** O4 (≥25% repeat rate, 100% on-time distributions) + O3 (understanding the payout = the true north-star).

---

## User & Situation (Q3)

**Persona:** Maya, the First-Job Wealth Builder (**Primary**)
**Situation:** Three weeks after her first investment, Maya gets a notification that rent has been distributed. She opens the app half-expecting to be confused or to find the number doesn't match what she was promised.

---

## Driving Forces (Q4)

**Hope:** To see her money actually working — real income landing, on schedule.

**Worry:** That the payout won't come, will be smaller than promised, or she won't understand where it came from.

---

## Device & Starting Point (Q5 + Q6)

**Device:** Mobile
**Entry:** Taps a push/email notification: "Your first rent distribution from 214 Oak St has arrived."

---

## Best Outcome (Q7)

**User Success:** Maya sees her exact share ("$3.42 this month"), understands it's rent minus disclosed costs, sees it matches the projection she was shown, and reads the monthly property update. She feels proof, not hope.

**Business Success:** A retained investor who trusts the income engine; a comprehension + satisfaction signal captured; setup for a repeat investment.

---

## Shortest Path (Q8)

1. **Home / Dashboard** — snapshot: total value, income to date, "How am I doing?" + a nudge that a distribution arrived.
2. **Portfolio** — sees her holding in 214 Oak St, its value and lifetime income.
3. **Income / Distributions** — the income calendar; this month's $3.42 broken down (gross rent → costs → her net share), matched against the original projection.
4. **Property Updates feed** — reads the monthly operator update (occupancy, reserves, anything notable) — present even when nothing dramatic happened. ✓

---

## Trigger Map Connections

**Persona:** Maya (Primary)

**Driving Forces Addressed:**
- ✅ **Want:** See her money grow; feel financially on-track.
- ❌ **Fear:** "Will I actually get paid?"; hidden risk she can't see.

**Business Goal:** O4 (retention/reliability) + O3 (understands the payout).
