## 5. Risk router

The router decides which conditional packs are loaded for a story. Positive triggers activate a pack only when they are tied to the story, planned diff, manifests, generated artifacts, or touched code. Negative triggers prevent shallow keyword matches from loading unrelated guidance. Minimum pack is the first slice to load; escalation triggers add specialist/high-assurance review; validation family names relevant check classes, not mandatory commands or proof claims.
