# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vesper serves two connected groups. The consumer product is for US retail investors aged roughly 22-40 who are comfortable with modern money apps but should not need to understand wallets, token standards, or Solana mechanics. They need to understand what they own, how income is generated, what can go wrong, and how liquidity actually works.

The admin and sponsor control plane is for compliance officers, diligence operators, sponsor principals, sponsor operations staff, and platform administrators. They need a reliable, auditable workflow for onboarding offerings, signing diligence gates, minting and listing assets, distributing funds, and reconciling off-chain records with on-chain state.

## Product Purpose

Vesper makes fractional, income-producing real estate understandable and verifiable while making it structurally difficult for an unqualified or fraudulent offering to reach investors. The consumer experience should make ownership, returns, risk, and exit constraints legible. The operational product should enforce human approval, segregation of duties, immutable auditability, and reconciliation across Convex and Solana.

Success means verified investors can fund an offering, understand the resulting ownership, receive accurate distributions, and verify the evidence behind the investment. It also means no offering reaches investors without every required human-signed gate and no privileged operation occurs without authorization and an audit trail.

## Brand Personality

Calm, radically transparent, and quietly premium. Vesper speaks in plain financial language, avoids crypto jargon in consumer workflows, states risk without alarmism, and becomes more communicative under stress. It should feel legally serious and operationally dependable rather than speculative or theatrical.

## Anti-references

- Crypto-casino interfaces, token hype, yield-farming language, glows, and gamified investment behavior.
- Generic fintech dashboards that obscure ownership, fees, risk, or liquidity behind polished aggregate metrics.
- Luxury real-estate brochures that substitute aspiration and lifestyle imagery for evidence and downside clarity.
- Institutional control planes that rely on memory, informal process, or visual warnings instead of enforcing authorization and segregation of duties.
- Any language or interaction that implies guaranteed yield, instant exits, AI approval authority, or unpermissioned trading.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the plain answer, then the breakdown, then the proof.
2. Make the safe and compliant action the easiest path through the interface.
3. Treat legal, risk, fee, and liquidity information as primary product content.
4. Keep blockchain mechanics verifiable but invisible until a user asks for proof.
5. Communicate state, failure, and recovery clearly at every high-stakes financial step.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA across consumer and admin surfaces. Core workflows must be keyboard operable, screen-reader legible, responsive on mobile and desktop, usable with reduced motion, and understandable without color as the only signal. Financial values use tabular numerals and explicit signs or labels. Focus states, errors, loading states, and transaction outcomes must remain clear for users with visual, motor, or cognitive accessibility needs.
