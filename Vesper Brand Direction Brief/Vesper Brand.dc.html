Brand mini-guide
1mOne-page do’s and don’ts + the story
Vesper Brand mini-guide · v1
Vesper is the evening star: a fixed, reliable point of light at dusk. Income that quietly works while you rest.
DO
Let white space and one indigo accent carry the design. Serif for headlines and brand moments; sans for every number. Use the warm star as a single, rare point of light. Make the mark work tiny, in grayscale, light and dark. Keep everything calm, premium, trustworthy. When in doubt, remove something.
DON’T
No web3-purple gradients, glows, or neon. No crypto clichés — 3D coins, rockets, degen energy. No second loud color; the star is a highlight, not a co-star. Never set financial numbers in the serif. Twilight, not midnight — never dark, heavy, or aggressive.
Type. Fraunces (display, opsz high) + Inter (UI/data, tabular figures). 1–2 fonts, no more. Motion. Eased, settled, no bounce. If anything twinkles, it’s the star — once, quietly. Imagery. Real properties, warm dusk light. No handshakes, no skyscraper glass, no coins.
